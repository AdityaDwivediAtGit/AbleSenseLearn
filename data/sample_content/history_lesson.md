# History Lesson: The Moon Landing

**Date:** July 20, 1969

## The Event
The **Apollo 11** mission was the spaceflight that first landed humans on the Moon. Commander **Neil Armstrong** and lunar module pilot **Buzz Aldrin** formed the American crew that landed the Apollo Lunar Module *Eagle*.

## Famous Quote
> "That's one small step for [a] man, one giant leap for mankind." - Neil Armstrong

## Significance
- Represented a major victory for the US in the Space Race.
- Advanced our understanding of the solar system.
- Demonstrated the capability of human engineering.
