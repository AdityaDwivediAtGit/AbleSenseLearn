AI-Powered Accessibility Features

Our platform adapts in real-time to support diverse learning needs

📝

Intelligent Text Simplification

AI automatically adjusts text complexity based on individual reading levels, making content accessible for students with dyslexia and other learning disabilities.

🖼️

Automatic Image Description

Computer vision AI generates detailed alt-text for images, diagrams, and charts, providing equal access to visual content for blind and low-vision students.

🎯

Personalized Learning Paths

Machine learning creates customized learning journeys that adapt to each student's pace, strengths, and challenges, ensuring optimal engagement and comprehension.

📊

Engagement Analytics

AI monitors learning patterns to detect frustration or disengagement, suggesting breaks or alternative content formats to maintain motivation.
